    Official Rules & Contest Terms

    1. Organizer & Overview

    The Montpellier Destination Languedoc Rail Voucher Contest (the "Contest") is organized by Rail Europe in partnership with Montpellier Destination Languedoc ("Organizers"). Entry into the Contest signifies full acceptance of these Terms and Conditions.

    2. Eligibility

    • The Contest is open to individuals aged 18 years or older at the time of entry.
    • Employees of Rail Europe, partner organizations, their immediate family members, and anyone professionally connected with the campaign are excluded from participating.
    • Void where prohibited by law.

    3. Entry Period & How to Enter

    • Entry Period: Begins on [START DATE] and closes on [END DATE] at 23:59 (CET).
    • How to Enter: Complete and submit the official entry form on the landing page during the Entry Period.
    • Limit: Strictly one (1) entry per person. Duplicate entries using different email addresses will result in disqualification. No purchase necessary to enter or win.

    4. Winner Selection & Notification

    • A total of 120 winners will be selected at random from all valid entries received during the Entry Period.
    • The random draw will take place within 15 business days following the closing date.
    • Winners will be notified via email at the address provided during entry. If a winner does not claim their prize or respond within 7 days, the Organizers reserve the right to select an alternate winner.

    5. Prize & Voucher Conditions

    • Prize: Each winner will receive one (1) Rail Europe digital voucher worth €50.
    • Validity: All vouchers must be redeemed on the Rail Europe platform by 31st December 2026 at 23:59 (CET). Expired vouchers cannot be renewed or extended.
    • Non-Refundable: Once issued or redeemed toward a booking, vouchers are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash, credit, or alternative prizes.
    • Non-Cumulative: Vouchers cannot be combined or accumulated with other promotional codes, coupons, discount vouchers, or special offers in a single transaction.

    6. Data Protection & Privacy

    Personal data collected during entry (e.g., name, email address) will be processed solely for administering the Contest and contacting winners. If you opt-in to receive marketing communications, Rail Europe will process your data in accordance with our Privacy Policy. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails.

    The Organizers accept no responsibility for lost, late, or misdirected entries due to technical glitches or internet network disruptions. This Contest is governed by the laws of France.
